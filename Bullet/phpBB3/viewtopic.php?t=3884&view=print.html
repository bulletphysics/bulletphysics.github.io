Polly 3D engine -> bulletphysics saved my life()

Posted: Wed Jul 29, 2009 8:41 am
by leuat
I've been mending my pet 3D project named "Polly"for quite some time, and originally planned to create a full-scale RPG game. However, being a coder and thus a very awkward 3D artist, I soon realized the project was too big solo. While visiting London this summer, I started to think about other more .. volatile game ideas. One hazardous night two weeks ago I implemented bullet physics. The course became clear: creating an arcade fast-paced vehicle shoot'em up game. With lots of innocents, huge explosions and whatnot.

A youtube video of the project can be seen here: http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Vwqsmz-MYs8

Thank you, bullet physics. You have made me a very happy person.
