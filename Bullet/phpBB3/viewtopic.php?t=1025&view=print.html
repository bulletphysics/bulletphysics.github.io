Bullet 2.46 is now available, and the Sweep and Prune broadphase can be switched to 32bit indices, allowing more objects.

Just enable

Code: Select all

#define BP_USE_FIXEDPOINT_INT_32 1
in Bullet/src/BulletCollision/btAxisSweep3.h

Hope this helps,
Erwin
