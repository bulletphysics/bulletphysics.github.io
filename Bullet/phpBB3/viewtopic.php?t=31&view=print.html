Bullet is currently developed as extern part of Blender, and lives in its sourcetree. Benefit of having it in an active development environment in this stage is maintenance of platform independence, and having a good testing framework. It can be tested in the gameengine.

cvs -z3 -d:pserver:anonymous@cvs.blender.org:/cvsroot/bf-projects co blender/extern/bullet

the CVS repository can be browsed online using ViewCVS:
http://projects.blender.org/viewcvs/vie ... bf-blender
