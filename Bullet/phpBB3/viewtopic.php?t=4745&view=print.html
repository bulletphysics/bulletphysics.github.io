Stinger Table Hockey was just announced today.


Stinger Table Hockey is a soon to be released full 3D recreation of table hockey, also known as bubble hockey and rod hockey. Developed independently by Stinger Games for the Apple iPhoneTM and Apple iPod Touch(R). Stinger Table Hockey features fantastic 3D graphics combined with Bullet Physics to provide a realistic and fun game of table hockey.
...
Bullet Physics provides a realistic, dynamic physics simulation that allows the puck to physically behave like a real puck, including sliding, spinning, bouncing up in the air, off the post, and even flying out of the arena. Used on next generation consoles, Bullet Physics has been harnessed to provide a unique gameplay experience for the iPhone and iPod Touch.
Threw in a plug for Bullet. It adds so much depth and life to the game.
