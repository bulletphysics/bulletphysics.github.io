Bullet 2.24 released

Posted: Thu Nov 09, 2006 5:01 am
by Erwin Coumans
Minor changes in the new Bullet 2.24 version:

- debugging for vehicle wheels (using the debugDrawer)
- improved vehicle demo (shifted the center of mass of the chassis for stability, other tweaks, still need lots of tuning before driving is fun)
- improved raytracer demo (can be used to visualize implicit collision shapes
- getUserPointer is a const method now
