Yet Another Space Shooter

Posted: Mon Mar 29, 2010 6:32 pm
by Ronin
We have released a new version of Yet Another Space Shooter.

With this techdemo we combine aspects of first person shooter and space simulation into a fine blended mixture. You can walk around on the surface of earth and mars as well as fly in space to visit our solar system. You can drive several vehicles, such as cars, hoovercrafts and spaceships. Weapons are included to serve your destructive mind. A carrier like spacecraft is in orbit of earth and can be commanded and walked upon.

Additionaly, there is an updater included, which will inform you of newly available modifications. Several RSS feeds on our webpage are available to keep you up to date.

Windows only. Please enjoy your stay.

You are welcomed to post anything related to this release at our project forum or here.

This game is using bullet for rigid body simulation in open space (thanks for double precision support!)
