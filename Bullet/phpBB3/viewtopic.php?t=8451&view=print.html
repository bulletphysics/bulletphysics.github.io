[FREE] Fantasy Dice (Android)

Posted: Tue Sep 25, 2012 3:03 pm
by finweltd
This app makes use of 3 different movement sensors and combines their data with a clever algorithm to create a stable, fast and accurate movement tracking system with your Android phone. When attached to Bullet physics modelling and HW accelerated 3D graphics, very compelling feel of control emerges where slight hand movements create immediate and natural looking visual feedback.

We have applied this technology to a dice app because it is a concept that everyone can understand and play with:

http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_4-WkSyFrZE

In short: Movements of the device (an Android phone) are tracked with sensor fusion, and applied to a box that is visualized on the screen. A few dice move freely inside the box, handled by Bullet physics. Glass cover prevents them from bouncing out of the box.





We are giving it away for FREE (and without advertisements), so if you have an Android device, download it from Google Play:
https://play.google.com/store/apps/deta ... antasydice

Have fun with it! This is our first app with Bullet physics, so we're looking for your feedback.
