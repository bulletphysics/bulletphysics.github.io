I just found out about quick baking.

ctrl + shift + alt + p

this renders frames = to your render settings (default 250)

it renders the ipos with out visulizations saving lots of time.

The only problem is you have to estimate how many frames are in your animation.

I have four scenes and my average is 4 minute running time (real time) and 4000 frames each. With all frames enabled on.

Normaly this takes about 6-8 hours to fully bake a scene. im getting 60 frames a minute here with the quick bake. Saving me tons of time. Just check your blender console window.

So instead of 6 hours i estimate just over a hour and half.

Yay. FOund thIs on the wiki



I hope this helps.
