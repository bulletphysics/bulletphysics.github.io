Hi Everyone,

Just a little message to tell you that we've just released a game prototype using Bullet.

The game's called "Black Death", here's the website : http://www.blackdeathgame.com/

The game uses our ToolChain called PlayAll wich can be seen here : http://www.playall.fr/

All Collisions System & Physics uses Bullet 2.78.

Here are some of the keyfeatures :

* PC, XBOX360 & PS3 Compatible
* Static, Dynamic & Keyframed collisions with complex channeling
* Static Collision Mesh Merger
* Triggering
* Collision Probe for gameplay coders (Raycasts, Swepts, Collides)
* Collision & Physical materials
* Physic Systems Editor (Bodies & Constraints)
* Ragdolls
* SoftBodies (Ropes & Cloth)

Hope you'll enjoy
