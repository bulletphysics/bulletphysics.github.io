Hi,

I'm new to the forum and thought I may as well show you what I'm working on.

I decided to use Bullet for my final year university project on '3D Modeling through Physical Simulation of Soft Bodies'. Basically I'm working on a system that allows you to 'grow' models by subdividing points and then changing the lengths of the springs connecting them. At the moment I'm still using the Bullet demos framework to develop. I've modified the renderer slightly to remove the gaps in the soft body rendering.
