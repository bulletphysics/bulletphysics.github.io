NaturalMotion is hiring physics developers (SF & Oxford)

Posted: Sat Dec 23, 2006 7:00 pm
by jobs
http://www.naturalmotion.com

NaturalMotion is looking to further expand its behavior authoring / game physics team responsible for the creation of cutting edge AI behaviors for interactive 3D character control.

We are recruiting candidates for this position who:

- Are comfortable with advanced engineering maths
- Have a familiarity with 3D animation tools
- Have a good eye for believable human motions
- Worked with articulated bodies and/or physical animation
- Experience with third party physics engines: (Havok, PhysX, ODE, Newton, Bullet, etc) or in-house / proprietary physics engines

We welcome candidates from an engineering or applied mathematical background with innovative ideas for synthesizing physical human behavior, as well as animators and TDs with a strong technical capability.

Successful applicants will be responsible for designing and implementing dynamically simulated characters and their real time controllers.

Location: San Francisco or Oxford (UK)

Mail resume/cover letter to:
careers@naturalmotion.com
