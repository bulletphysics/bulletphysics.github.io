Some of the physics related sessions during this years Game Developers Conference 2008 in San Francisco.
  • Physics Tutorial by Jim Van Verth,Erin Catto, Squirrel Eiserloh, Marq Singer, Gino van den Bergen, Christer Ericson, Erwin Coumans
  • Creating Havok with Destruction Sponsored by Havok by Oliver Strunk, Havok.
  • Never Say Die: Breathing life into Game Characters with Havok Behavior by Jeff Yates, Havok
  • Implementing Ragdoll Physics on the Nintendo DS by Eric Brown.
  • MERCENARIES 2: Networked Physics in a Large Streaming World by Glenn Fiedler.
  • Momentum vs. Character Animation by David Wu.
There will be some Bullet/Physics demos at the GDC showfloor, more details follow.

Please reply if there are other relevant sessions, or if you are planning to join/meet up!
