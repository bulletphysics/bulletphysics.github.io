Hi!

I am Ignacio Sanchez from Geardome.

We are about to release a game for iPhone and iPod touch called Ozone, that uses Bullet for its physic simulation.

The game, mix of an action and a puzzle game, is highly detailed and complex in comparison with the average games on the Store. We have taken care of every aspect of its development, including smooth visuals and a crystal clear sound track. This sound track is one of its big points and can be downloaded freely from the website.

We are very proud of how bullet has been integrated into the game and the benefit of letting us focus in the gameplay.

Bullet runs quite good on iPhone and we are planning to keep using it in future games. We want to thank the developers behind bullet for such a great free library!

Ozone will be out on April 9th on the App Store. Give it a try!
