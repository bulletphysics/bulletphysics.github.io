Stunt Rally

Posted: Fri Aug 21, 2015 6:55 am
by CryHam
Stunt Rally started from using VDrift simulation in 2010, which already used Bullet.
It mainly features rally racing cars on gravel roads and tracks with various stunts
(jumps, loops, pipes, obstacles, dynamic objects, water, mud).

Recently it also included simple hovering spaceships and a bouncing sphere.
Current version on master has now also a simple bike and a 6 wheeled vehicle WIP.

Everything including wiki, downloads, gallery, videos and more is on our website:
http://stuntrally.tuxfamily.org/

Have fun.
