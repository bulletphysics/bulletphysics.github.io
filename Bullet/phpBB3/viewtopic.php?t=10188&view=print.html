We recently released Beach Buggy Racing on iOS (AppStore) and Android (Google Play, Amazon), soon to be released on Windows Store/Phone as well. Split-screen Kart racing on Android TV and FireTV!



Trailer: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pkxE_3WqUR8
More Info: http://www.vectorunit.com/beach-buggy-racing

As usual, this game was made possible by using Bullet for collision detection and rigid body simulation. A lot of the power-ups/weapons are physics-based (e.g. Low Gravity, Ball and Chain, Big Tires, Spring, Tornado, Spiked Tires, etc).
