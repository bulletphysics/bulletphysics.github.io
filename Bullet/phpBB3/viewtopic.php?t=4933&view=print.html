PhD Student Position in Computational Science and Engineering - Umeå University, Sweden.

We are looking for an exceptional PhD student to join our interdisciplinary group focusing on modeling and
numerical simulation of complex mechanical systems. This is a full-time position over a period of four years.
The PhD student will contribute to the project “Modeling and Simulation of Large-scale Granular Matter with
Complex Flows” aiming at developing new models, algorithms and numerical computing techniques.

The application deadline is April 16.
