First you need to change the ratio between the rope and the axle. Note the following systems that reduces the turning ratio (i.e. a large steering wheel turn equals a smaller axle/wheel movement: http://www.leany.com/images/types_final.jpg

Next you need axle stops to prevent the axle from excessive turning (i.e. "flipping" your racer). Note the "bolts with nuts" on either side of the axle: http://aasbd.com/docs/Stock_Plans.pdf.
