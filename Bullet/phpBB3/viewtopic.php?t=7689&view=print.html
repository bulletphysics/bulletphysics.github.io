Hi,

- Physics for game programmers by Grant Palmer
Accessible and not too complex

- Physics For Dummies by Steve Holzner
Not kidding, it's pretty good to get your head around some concepts in physics or refresh your memory.

- Real-time collision detection by Christer Ericson
Everything you need to know about collision detection

- Physics based animation by Kenny Erleben, Jon Sporring, Knud Henriksen and Henrik Dohlmann
Really advanced, I would not recommend this for a beginner
