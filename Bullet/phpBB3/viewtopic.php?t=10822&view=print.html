Presumably your 'car' needs some downforce, but not generated from aerodynamics like a real car, otherwise you'd probably float off at zero speed. Make sure normal gravity is off (which is a force vector relative to the world), and then apply some sort of replacement force vector always pushing your car downwards (relative to the car itself).
