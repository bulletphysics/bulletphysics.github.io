Bullet 2.23 released. improved penetration depth estimation

Posted: Sat Nov 04, 2006 5:32 am
by Erwin Coumans
Bullet 2.23 is available.

- Improved penetration depth estimation by sampling preferred directions. Currently penetrations involving boxes or triangles are improved: they report the triangle normal and box face normals.
- Some constraint solver experiments (randomizing the contact points)
- btMotionState is required now to synchronize world transform
- btCollisionObject is a class, member have accessors (sorry for the API changes)
- solid rendering of meshes (graphics)
