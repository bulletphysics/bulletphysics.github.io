My game using Bullet has just been approved on the App Store. And I am very exited about it.

You basically shape your pet using blocks of various size. It then uses some Genetics Algorithms to compute a movement that will give it life and let it walk. You will love to shape your pet with only a few leg and see him actually using it. Or give it a snake shape and see it crawl.

It's called Learning Pets and available for the iPad here: http://itunes.apple.com/gb/app/learning ... d530192045.
